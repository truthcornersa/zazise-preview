Privacy (demo)

How we handle personal information in this ZAZISE early adopter demo. It is clear, proportionate, and written for South Africans.

1. Who we are

For this demo, “we” means ZAZISE (South Africa) operating the early adopter wireframe kit and local registration flow. Contact for demo related privacy questions should go through the channel by which you were invited, or the project maintainers named in that invite.

2. What we collect

When you register as an early adopter in this demo, we may collect:

  • First name
  • Surname
  • Email address
  • Password, stored only as a hashed value (never shown as plaintext in notices, logs meant for operators, or emails)

We also use a human verification step (reCAPTCHA / similar) so bots don’t flood the list. That provider may process technical signals under its own terms.

What we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. Early adopter data exists to run this demo and to notify you about ZAZISE, not to trade.

3. Why we collect it (purpose)

  • To maintain an early adopter list of people who registered interest.
  • To notify you about preview progress, invites, or next steps (where an email was provided).
  • To operate the local demo (authentication / account shape in the sample build).
  • To show preview personalisation such as profile initials on the Early Adopter Preview home.

4. Where it is stored

This build stores registration records in a local demo database / file on the machine running the demo (for example a local JSON store under the wireframes data/ folder). It is not a production cloud launch. Treat the host machine’s access controls as the primary safeguard for this demo.

Passwords are retained only in hashed form. Plaintext passwords are not written into notices for operators.

5. Cookies, localStorage & preview initials

The Early Adopter Preview may keep a small profile object (e.g. first name, surname, and derived initials) in browser localStorage / sessionStorage so the avatar can greet you without a full production auth stack. Language preference may also be stored locally (e.g. zazise.lang).

You can clear this data by clearing site data for the demo origin in your browser.

6. Retention

For this demo, we keep early adopter records for as long as they are useful to the initiative (notify / acknowledge / shape the product), or until you ask us to remove your details where practically possible on the demo host. When the demo is retired, local stores should be wiped or archived only as needed for the project’s internal lessons, not for secondary marketing unrelated to ZAZISE.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Limited technical sharing may occur with verification providers (e.g. reCAPTCHA) solely to run the demo’s abuse prevention step. Contributors acknowledged in project materials are named with care; your registration email is not published as a “contributor list” by default.

8. Your choices

  • Use a contact email you’re comfortable receiving project updates on.
  • Ask (via your invite channel) to be removed from the early adopter list where the demo host can action it.
  • Clear browser storage to remove local preview initials and language prefs.

9. South Africa & POPIA (overview)

We aim to handle personal information in a way that respects the spirit of South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA): collect only what we need for the early adopter purpose, keep it secure for a demo context, be transparent, and avoid secondary use that would surprise you. This paragraph gives a broad overview of our approach.

10. Contact

Privacy questions about this demo: use the same channel that invited you into the early adopter programme, addressed to ZAZISE (South Africa).
