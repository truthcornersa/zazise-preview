Welcome, early adopter

A short guide to what ZAZISE is, why this demo exists, and how to try the current build, with warmth, clarity, and a little South African soul.

Born from love of South Africa

ZAZISE grew out of a simple conviction: South Africa deserves a video space that sounds like home, not a copy of somewhere else. We want a place where creators, storytellers, and everyday voices can Make Yourself Known in the languages we actually speak.

That means designing for up to 12 South African languages (including South African Sign Language as a prominent presence in the UI), celebrating local craft, and building with respect for the people who will use it. ZAZISE carries that voice: premium, minimal, warm, with craft over noise.

Public participation. This early adopter initiative invites South Africans (and friends of SA) to register interest, poke at the preview, and help shape what ships. Contributors who put time, feedback, or craft into the journey are acknowledged with gratitude. You are part of the story, not just the audience.

What you’re looking at

This is a local early adopter demo, not the live product or a production launch. It exists so you can feel the flow: register, then thank you, then Early Adopter Preview home.

The current build: what to try

Register First name, surname, email, password (with confirm), and reCAPTCHA. Local demo only.
Thank you You’re on the list. Early adopter confirmation includes a clear path into the preview.
Hover GIFs Trend and video cards come alive on hover, a taste of SA motion and mood.
Top Trending Several colour options and hover accents (gold / blue / green) bring craft, not chrome.
Cooking toasts “We’re still cooking. Be patient.” Friendly toasts when preview CTAs aren’t live yet.
Logo → register Tap the ZAZISE mark from the preview to head back to registration.

Profile initials

After you register with a first name and surname, the preview avatar shows initials derived from those two fields (e.g. Thabo Nkosi → TN). Initials are kept in browser storage for the demo preview. See the Privacy note.

How to try it locally

  1. Open a terminal in the wireframes folder: /workspace/zazise-wireframes
  2. Install and start the demo server: npm install then npm start
  3. Visit http://127.0.0.1:8787/register.html and create an early adopter account (name, surname, email, password, reCAPTCHA).
  4. After success you’ll land on the thank you screen, then open Early Adopter Preview to explore the home experience.
  5. Hover cards, try Top Trending colours, click around. Cooking toasts will gently remind you that some paths are still simmering.

As an early adopter

You’re not “just testing software.” You’re helping decide what a SA first video space should feel like. Share what delights you, what confuses you, and what you’d want your community to see first. We listen.

  • Register once with a real email you’re happy to hear from later.
  • Use the language bar: EN, AF, ZU, XH, ST, and more, then tell us what reads true.
  • Treat the preview as a conversation starter, not a finished product.

Need a hand?

For this demo, start with the README in the wireframes kit, or reach out through the channel your invite came from. Keep feedback kind and concrete. That’s how craft gets better.
